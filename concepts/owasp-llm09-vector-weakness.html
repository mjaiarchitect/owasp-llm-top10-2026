LLM09 — Vector and Embedding Weaknesses

← Back
Details (click to expand)

Attacks that depend on embedding-space geometry and similarity search: cross-tenant leakage when search runs before ACL, embedding inversion of “vectors-only” backups, retrieval-time geometric poisoning, jamming, or membership inference from scores. Enforce tenant scope inside the index query; treat embeddings like source documents.

How hacker manage to do that?

They craft similarity queries or poisoned vectors that retrieve another tenant’s chunks or invert embeddings from a “vectors-only” store. Watch retrieval that runs before ACL filters and indexes that mix tenants or skip document sanitization before embed.

Diagram (aligned BEFORE → AFTER columns): compare similarity search across all tenants before ACL with sanitization, tenant scope inside the index query, and authorization before return. Gold NEW highlights mark controls added outside the model; the LLM brain sees foreign chunks in BEFORE, but only authorized chunks—or a denial—in AFTER.
