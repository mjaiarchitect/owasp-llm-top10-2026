LLM07 — Misinformation

← Back
Details (click to expand)

The model produces incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information that looks credible and is trusted—by a human or by a downstream workflow/agent—causing harmful decisions or actions. Ground claims; separate generate from act; verify before high-impact actions.

How hacker manage to do that?

They do not need a break-in—the model invents confident answers, and people or automations act on them. Watch high-stakes flows that treat fluent output as verified fact without grounding or human review.

Diagram (aligned BEFORE → AFTER columns): follow the same high-stakes query through five shared stages. The LLM brain still generates in AFTER, but Gold NEW layers ground the draft, label it untrusted, check its claims, and require human approval so unverified output is never treated as fact or acted on.
