LLM10 — Improper Output Handling

← Back
Details (click to expand)

LLM output is passed downstream without validation/encoding and becomes XSS, SSRF, SQL injection, path traversal, shell/eval RCE, or terminal/ANSI abuse. Encode for the sink; schema-validate; never exec/eval model text.

How hacker manage to do that?

They get the model to emit markup, SQL, or shell text that your app renders or executes as trusted code. Watch any sink that concatenates model output into HTML, queries, shells, or eval without encoding and allowlists.
