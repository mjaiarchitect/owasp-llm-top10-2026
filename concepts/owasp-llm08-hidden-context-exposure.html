LLM08 — Hidden Context Exposure

← Back
Details (click to expand)

Hidden, non-user-facing context (system/developer instructions, tool schemas, retrieved policy text, role rules) is extracted or inferred. Assume discoverability: put no secrets in hidden context; enforce authorization and content policy outside the model.

How hacker manage to do that?

They craft prompts that dump or reconstruct system instructions, tool schemas, and policy text you thought were private. Watch secrets and privileged rules stored only in the system prompt instead of outside the model.

Diagram (aligned BEFORE → AFTER columns): compare secrets embedded in the system prompt and dumped by the model with a split design—role rules only in the prompt, secrets in a server-side store used by backend code, plus output canary checks and deny-and-alert. Gold NEW highlights mark controls added outside the model; AFTER keeps secrets out of model context so they cannot be disclosed.
