LLM04 — Supply Chain

Details (click to expand)

Integrity failures in third-party models, datasets, adapters, conversion/merge/quantization pipelines, packages, or serving stacks (lookalike Hub models, unsigned LoRA, mutable latest tags). Vet suppliers; pin immutable digests; sign artifacts; maintain SBOM/AIBOM (Software Bill of Materials / AI Bill of Materials).

How hacker manage to do that?

They publish lookalike models, unsigned adapters, or tampered packages that CI pulls via a mutable tag or unverified download. Watch every third-party artifact you ingest without pinning, signing, and supplier vetting.
